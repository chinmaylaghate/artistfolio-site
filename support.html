Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account to use ArtistFolio?

No. You can browse all artwork without signing in. An iCloud account is only required to save art, follow artists, or publish your work.

How do I buy artwork?

ArtistFolio does not process payments. Tap the contact button on any artwork or artist profile to reach the artist directly and arrange a purchase.

Why can't I see the AR preview?

AR preview requires a device with world-tracking AR support running iOS 17 or later. On unsupported devices, the button is hidden.

I published artwork but it's not showing in the feed.

New listings can take a minute or two to appear in the public feed. Pull to refresh the Home tab. If it still doesn't appear after a few minutes, check your Studio to confirm it saved correctly.

How do I remove a saved artwork or unfollow an artist?

In the Saved tab, swipe left on any saved item to remove it. On an artist's profile, tap the Following button to unfollow.

Is my data private?

Yes. ArtistFolio stores private data (your saved art, follows) in your personal iCloud database — only you can see it. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
