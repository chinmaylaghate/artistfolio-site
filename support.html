Caring for Your Artwork

General advice for looking after original art. If the artist gave their own care note, follow that first.

Oil on Canvas
  • Hang out of direct sunlight to keep colours bright.
  • Dust with a soft, dry brush. Avoid cloths, water and sprays.
  • Keep away from radiators, fireplaces and damp walls.
  • Lift by the frame or stretcher, never the painted surface.
Acrylic
  • Hang out of direct sunlight to keep colours bright.
  • Dust with a soft, dry brush. No cloths or sprays.
  • Keep away from damp walls, kitchens and bathrooms.
  • Lift by the frame or stretcher, not the painted surface.
Watercolor
  • Keep out of direct sunlight; watercolours fade fastest.
  • Frame behind UV-filtering glass with an acid-free mount.
  • Hang away from humidity to stop the paper rippling.
  • Handle unframed works by the edges with clean, dry hands.
Pastel
  • Keep framed under glass with a spacer so nothing touches the surface.
  • Never touch or brush the surface; pastel smudges easily.
  • Carry and store it flat and upright, without knocks.
  • Hang out of direct sunlight and away from damp.
Digital
  • Hang out of direct sunlight to stop the print fading.
  • Frame behind UV-filtering glass for the longest life.
  • Keep away from moisture and steam.
  • Handle unframed prints by the edges, ideally with cotton gloves.
Mixed Media
  • Hang out of direct sunlight to protect every layer.
  • Dust gently with a soft, dry brush. No liquids.
  • Keep temperature and humidity steady so layers stay bonded.
  • Hold by the edges or frame; raised parts can be fragile.
Sculpture
  • Place on a stable, level surface away from busy walkways.
  • Dust with a soft brush or dry microfibre cloth.
  • Avoid water, polish and cleaning products unless the artist advises.
  • Lift from the base with both hands, never by thin parts.
Photography
  • Hang out of direct sunlight to prevent fading.
  • Frame behind UV-filtering glass with an acid-free mount.
  • Keep away from humidity and steam.
  • Hold prints by the edges; fingerprints mark the surface.
Printmaking
  • Keep out of direct sunlight to protect the inks and paper.
  • Frame with acid-free mounts and UV-filtering glass.
  • Hang away from damp to prevent foxing and rippling.
  • Handle unframed prints by the margins with clean hands.
Drawing
  • Keep framed under glass; graphite and charcoal smudge.
  • Hang out of direct sunlight to stop the paper yellowing.
  • Keep away from humidity and damp walls.
  • Never touch the drawn surface; hold by the edges.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account to use ArtistFolio?

No. You can browse all artwork without signing in. An iCloud account is only required to save art, follow artists, or publish your work.

How do I buy artwork?

ArtistFolio does not process payments. Tap the contact button on any artwork or artist profile to reach the artist directly and arrange a purchase.

Why can't I see the AR preview?

AR preview requires a device with world-tracking AR support running iOS 17 or later. On unsupported devices, the button is hidden.

I published artwork but it's not showing in the feed.

New listings can take a minute or two to appear in the public feed. Pull to refresh the Home tab. If it still doesn't appear after a few minutes, check your Studio to confirm it saved correctly.

How do I remove a saved artwork or unfollow an artist?

In the Saved tab, swipe left on any saved item to remove it. On an artist's profile, tap the Following button to unfollow.

Is my data private?

Yes. ArtistFolio stores private data (your saved art, follows) in your personal iCloud database — only you can see it. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
