Features

Everything between
a first look and a sale.

01

Discover original work

Featured collections, featured artists and just-listed pieces on one calm Home. Browse as a guest, no sign-in.

02

Search by style

Browse collections from abstract to old masters, or search by artist, medium or style.

03

See it on your wall

Tap View on Wall and point your camera at any wall. AR shows the piece at its real size before you decide.

04

Follow and contact artists

Follow artists to see their new work first, and reach them by email, Instagram or their website. You arrange payment and delivery together.

05

Your own Studio

Artists publish a piece with photos, a title and a price in minutes, and manage every listing in one place.

06

Saved, in your iCloud

Saved artworks and the artists you follow live in your profile, stored in your private iCloud.
